Pick a consumer product and the channels it is offered through, answer the questions that apply, and see which typologies are linked to it. Fit is relevance by exposure overlap, not a risk rating. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere. Your answers are written to this page's address (the part after #, so you can copy the link) and, as one short text value, to this browser's local storage on this device. Nothing is sent to a server. A fresh visit starts blank: if saved answers exist you are offered a button to resume them, or to forget them.
