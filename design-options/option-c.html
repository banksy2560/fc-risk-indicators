About: how linking works

Typologies link to canonical indicators; each link names its controls. Every indicator and control is defined once and linked many times, so open any result to move in both directions: typology to indicators to controls, or control back to the indicators and typologies it serves. Severity is indicative signal strength and is never combined into a score. The typology is the unit of analysis.
