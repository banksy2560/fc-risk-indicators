How linking works

A typology is a recognisable way a financial crime is carried out. It links to the indicators you would observe if it were happening, such as rapid pass-through of received funds.

Each of those links names the controls that mitigate that indicator in that typology. Indicators and controls are defined once and linked many times, so the same control can serve several indicators and several typologies. The reuse count on each node shows how many typologies share it.

The linkage view reads in both directions: pick a typology to see its indicators and controls, or search an indicator or control to see every typology it appears in.

How the product mapper works, and its limits

The mapper covers a consumer bank: seven products (everyday account, savings account, term deposit, credit card, personal loan, home loan fixed and variable) and four channels (mobile app, internet banking, phone banking, retail network). You pick one product and the channels it is offered through; only the questions that apply to that product and channel are shown. Each typology carries AI-inferred consumer products and channels, and the typologies linked to your product and at least one of your channels form the base set. Your answers map to payment rails and customer segments, and a typology is a strong fit when both overlap its exposure, a possible fit when one does, and a base fit when only product and channel match. Retail customers is treated as true of every consumer product, so it does not count. Each result shows which tags matched and which answers implied them. Cross-cutting typologies are listed separately as applying across all products.

  • This is relevance by exposure overlap. It is not a risk rating and there is no score. The institution assesses applicability, inherent risk, control effectiveness and residual risk in its own process.
  • Exposure tags and the answer-to-tag mapping are AI-inferred and unreviewed. A missing match does not mean a typology is irrelevant; a match does not mean it applies.
  • Example profiles are illustrative, not real products. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • What is stored, and where. Your selected answers, manual includes and excludes, chosen example profile and tab are kept in two places in your own browser: the page address after the # (so a link reproduces your view) and one short text value in this browser's local storage under the key fc-product-v2. Nothing is sent to a server, and there are no cookies or analytics. Opening /product without answers in the address starts blank; if a saved value exists you are offered "Resume your last assessment on this device" or "Forget saved answers", which deletes it. Reset, or answering afresh, also replaces it. Clearing site data in your browser removes it too. On a shared computer, use Forget saved answers when you finish.

Citation status tags

Each typology source carries a tag. No citation has been confirmed by a human.

  • agent-reviewed An AI agent searched for it and found a matching real document. A listed link may not have been fetched. Still verify it.
  • corrected, agent-reviewed The original title, year or publisher was wrong and was corrected by the same process.
  • unverified corrected, unverified Not confirmed by search; from model knowledge.
  • too vague, unverified Names a body or topic but not a specific document.
  • replacement suggested, unverified The original could not be found; a nearest real document is suggested.
