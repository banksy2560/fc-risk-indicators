Typology by control family

Every typology against the 19 control families. Each cell counts the distinct controls from that family linked to the typology. Select a cell, a typology name or a family heading to see the controls behind the number.

Read this first. Counts show linkage, NOT effectiveness or risk. A darker cell means more controls are linked in the library. It does not mean the typology is well controlled, adequately covered or low risk, and an empty cell is not necessarily a gap. This is v0.1 draft content that has not been reviewed.
